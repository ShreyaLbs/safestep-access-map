Urgency Score Formula

urgency = (severityWeight × confirmations) + recencyBoost − resolvedPenalty
  • severityWeight: High = 3 · Medium = 2 · Low = 1
  • recencyBoost: max 15 pts, halves every 7 days
  • resolvedPenalty: −100 (pushes resolved items to bottom)

This formula auto-ranks issues so the most severe, most community-confirmed, and most recent reports always surface to the top — giving campus and civic teams a clear action order.
